# European Portuguese Content Guide

## Scope

All learner-facing Portuguese defaults to contemporary European Portuguese (`pt-PT`) as used in Portugal. Brazilian Portuguese (`pt-BR`) must not be used as the unmarked default.

This guide is a quality gate, not a substitute for a native European Portuguese reviewer.

## Content Status

Every learning item has one of these states:

```text
draft -> pt-PT-reviewed -> teaching-reviewed -> audio-approved -> published
```

- AI-generated or machine-translated content always begins as `draft`.
- Only `published` content may appear in production learner flows.
- The reviewer, review date, and important notes must be recorded.

## Language Rules

- Set the locale explicitly to `pt-PT` in content and speech tooling.
- Use current European Portuguese spelling consistently unless a scenario intentionally teaches variation.
- Prefer language a person would naturally use in the documented setting and relationship.
- Record register for each item: `casual`, `neutral`, or `formal`.
- Treat `tu`, omitted subjects, `você`, and forms such as `o senhor` or `a senhora` as context-sensitive choices. Do not apply one form universally.
- Prefer the standard European Portuguese progressive construction, such as `estou a falar`, unless regional or contextual variation is intentionally taught.
- Do not translate English idioms literally without confirming natural usage.
- Contractions, discourse markers, and reduced spoken forms need an explanation when the written and heard forms differ materially for learners.

## Initial Variant Checks

These examples are review warnings, not a complete banned-word list:

| Default `pt-PT` context | Common `pt-BR` alternative to flag |
| --- | --- |
| autocarro | ônibus |
| comboio | trem |
| telemóvel | celular |
| casa de banho | banheiro |
| pequeno-almoço | café da manhã |
| sumo | suco |

A flagged term may be valid in a quoted, comparative, regional, or Lusophone context, but that intent must be explicit.

## Required Item Metadata

Learning content should record:

- Communicative goal and situation.
- Approximate learner level.
- Speaker relationship and register.
- Portuguese text and meaning in English.
- Key chunks and acceptable response alternatives.
- Common misunderstandings or unnatural responses.
- Locale and regional information.
- Content status, reviewer, and review date.
- Audio speaker, accent/region where known, source, and usage rights.

## Audio

- Core production dialogue should ultimately use approved European Portuguese speech.
- Prototype text-to-speech must be labeled as provisional and configured for `pt-PT`.
- Do not represent synthetic pronunciation scoring as definitive.
- Preserve natural speed and prosody; assisted versions may slow or segment audio without replacing the natural version.

## Sources and Rights

- Write original teaching dialogues unless a source license clearly permits reuse.
- Record source, license, attribution, and modifications for reused material.
- Do not copy competitor lessons, dialogue libraries, translations, or recordings.
- Corpora and public references may inform authenticity, but their individual licenses must be checked before material is distributed.

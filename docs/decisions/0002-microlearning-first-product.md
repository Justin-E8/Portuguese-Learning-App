# Decision 0002: Microlearning-First Product

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-11-24

## Context

The initial product direction centered on immersive Conversation Missions supported by smaller practice activities. After reconsidering how and when learners are likely to use the app, the product should prioritize activities that can be completed quickly and independently.

## Decision

Make short microlearning activities the core product experience. The initial activity families are:

- Listening Bites.
- Phrase and vocabulary review.
- Sentence practice.
- Response practice.
- Mixed one-, three-, and five-minute review sessions.

Interactive stories and long Conversation Missions are removed from the MVP direction.

All activity families should be generated from a shared, reviewed European Portuguese content library so that one word, phrase, sentence, or prompt can be practiced in several ways.

## Consequences

- The first content model should be item- and activity-centered rather than story-centered.
- Progress can be stored per skill, such as listening recognition, written recognition, recall, and response selection.
- Users can choose a topic or activity without completing earlier narrative content.
- Content can be developed and validated in smaller batches.
- The universal Expo architecture remains appropriate and does not need to change.

## Revisit When

- Learner testing shows that longer contextual activities materially improve retention or engagement.
- The short-activity model lacks enough context for intermediate or advanced learners.
